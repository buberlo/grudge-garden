# Grudge Garden

> Water your petty slights, prune your rage, harvest a very small victory.

A casual strategy game grows petty slights as plants with needs based on who offended you. Overwatering makes resentment toxic, so you must balance apologies, gossip, and composted drama. Your past self visits the garden and only pays tribute if the plants look appropriately bitter.

## Features
- Plant slights by choosing offense type, severity, and offender
- Care minigames for watering, pruning, and passive-aggressive mulching
- Grief blooms that grant power but require feeding with new petty insults
- Past self visits that score your garden on bitterness aesthetics

## Stack
- Godot
- GDScript
- Howler.js

## Getting started
```
Open the project in Godot 4 and press F5; include Howler.js assets for web export audio support.
```
